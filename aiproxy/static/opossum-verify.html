verify a receipt

someone shared an opossum disclosure with you. this checks, in your browser, that the opossum relay signed it and that nothing shown was changed. it reveals only what they chose to share.

how this works

  • each receipt is a jwt signed by the relay with ed25519. its public key is at /opossum/.well-known/jwks.json.
  • every field inside is only a salted sha-256 digest. a shared field comes with its salt, so its digest can be recomputed and matched; unshared fields stay sealed.
  • a "proven note" is checked the same way: the note and its salt must hash to the commitment the relay signed at payment time.
  • receipts marked test money came from the sandbox. no real money moved.
  • anything labelled "stated by the payer" is their own description and is not signed.
